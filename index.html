Quack

Loading quantum engine (Pyodide + Qiskit) and sending one circuit to the quantum backend. This can take up to 30 s.

Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, E or Enter to use a terminal, Space to run the circuit, Tab to switch character (1 player), R to reset the stage, Esc for the menu.

Quack

A quantum puzzle for one or two players. Shared states, classical messages and a path through the core.

Level

Controls

  • 1 player: WASD or arrow keys move the active character, E or Enter uses a terminal, Tab switches between Alice and Bob.
  • 2 players: Alice uses WASD and E. Bob uses the arrow keys and Enter.
  • Anywhere in a level: Space runs the circuit (levels that ask for it), R resets the current stage, M mutes or unmutes sound, Esc returns to the menu.
  • Gate monitors: stand on your terminal and use E / Enter. Shared CX needs both players on its plates.
