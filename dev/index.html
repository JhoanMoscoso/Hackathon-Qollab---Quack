Entangled

A two-player quantum puzzle. Alice and Bob share one entangled state: what one measures, the other learns.

Controls

  • 1 player: WASD or arrow keys move the active character, E or Enter interacts, Tab switches between Alice and Bob.
  • 2 players: Alice uses WASD and E. Bob uses the arrow keys and Enter.
  • Anywhere in a level: R restarts the level, Esc returns to the menu.
